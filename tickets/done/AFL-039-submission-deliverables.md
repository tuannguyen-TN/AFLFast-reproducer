Open issue, awaiting a lead ruling (stop condition under the scope rule). The
orchestrator read `harness/run_campaign.py` at HEAD a23c569 and found:
- `--kind main` accepts only `experiments/configs/main.yaml` and needs at least 10 trials.

- Every other non-main kind runs `PILOT_TRIALS = 3` trials per arm and ignores `trials_per_config`.
- `harness/aggregate.py` and `harness/plots.py` analyse only `"kind": "main"` campaigns.

So B1's "1 trial each, 60 s" plus an analysis step cannot be met with the CLI
unchanged. B1, B2, B3 (the demo commands) and the analysis step in C are blocked
until the lead rules.
